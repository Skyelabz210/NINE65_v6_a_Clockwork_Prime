## MATHEMATICAL RIGOR

### Formal Theorems Included

1. **K-Free Polynomial Representation Theorem**
   - Overflow k implicit in phase relationships
   - Exact reconstruction without explicit k-tracking
   - Applies to polynomials in any ring

2. **Persistent Montgomery Correctness Theorem**
   - Operations in Montgomery form produce same results as standard
   - Negacyclic convolution exact in persistent form
   - Proof by induction on NTT stages

3. **Bootstrap-Free Guarantee Theorem**
   - Static parameter selection guarantees noise never exceeds threshold
   - Valid for any finite-depth polynomial circuit
   - Applies to BFV, BGV, CKKS, TFHE

4. **Integer Noise Additivity Theorem**
   - Millibits representation is lossless for noise operations
   - Zero drift in noise accumulation
   - Mathematically equivalent to exact log₂ tracking
